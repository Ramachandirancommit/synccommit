# Application visuals

All images under `public/images/` are original sample SVG illustrations stored in this repository. They may be used, modified, and kept permanently in this application without image-service subscriptions, attribution requirements, or remote image requests. The logo is a custom SyncCommit mark, also used as the favicon.

People avatars depict fictional collaboration roles, not actual employees or customers. Project images depict sample interfaces and concepts. Location icons do not imply an office address.

Interface icons use the existing `lucide-react` package, which permits personal and commercial use. Its ISC and inherited Feather MIT notices are included in `public/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt` and must be retained when redistributing the icons. Official license: https://lucide.dev/license

Page illustrations are configured in `src/config/content.js`. The shared `PhotoSlot` component supplies a local sample illustration when a source is absent or fails to load. No real photographs are required.
